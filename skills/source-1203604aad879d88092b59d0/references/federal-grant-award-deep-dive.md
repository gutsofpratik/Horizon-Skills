# Federal Grant Award Deep Dive

Use this reference for one resolved federal grant or assistance award.

## Default procedure

1. **Lock the award.** Capture exact ID, recipient, agency, program/assistance listing, assistance type, dates, amount fields, place, purpose, and direct relationships.
2. **Classify the instrument and value.** Keep award amount, current federal action, total funding, cost share, and program ceiling distinct.
3. **Resolve the recipient.** Use exact recipient/vendor identity and avoid silent parent, affiliate, or similarly named expansion.
4. **Follow the program.** Expand the linked grant program when it changes eligibility, recurring funding, statutory purpose, or recipient landscape.
5. **Recover the originating opportunity.** Prefer exact linked notice/program identifiers and read source files when eligibility, reporting, or cost share matters.
6. **Analyze funding history.** Use modifications or transactions to explain timing and funding changes without double counting.
7. **Review subawards when relevant.** Separate prime-recipient funding from subrecipient activity.
8. **Add bounded comparisons.** Use same-program, same-agency, or same-purpose awards only to answer a specific benchmark or landscape question.

## Output contract

Return:

- award identity, recipient, funder, purpose, dates, and value basis
- program and opportunity lineage
- funding and modification timeline
- recipient and subaward posture
- renewal, continuation, partnership, or market implications requested
- confidence and evidence gaps

## Gotchas

- Assistance awards are not procurement contracts.
- Award amount, obligated action, program ceiling, and recipient revenue are different concepts.
- A recipient match must use exact identity where possible.
- Similar programs or descriptions are analogs unless exact linkage exists.
