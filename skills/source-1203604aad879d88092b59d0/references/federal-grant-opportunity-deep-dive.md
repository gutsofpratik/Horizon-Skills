# Federal Grant Opportunity Deep Dive

Use this reference for one resolved federal funding opportunity.

## Default procedure

1. **Lock the opportunity.** Capture exact ID, notice/funding number, agency, program, status, dates, funding range, assistance type, eligibility, geography, and files.
2. **Classify actionability.** Use current date, closing date, amendments, and later notices. A past deadline is not an active application window.
3. **Read controlling files.** Follow [Vector-store content retrieval](govtribe-docs-vector-store-content-retrieval.md) with `Add_To_Vector_Store` and `Search_Vector_Store` when exact eligibility, cost share, scoring, forms, reporting, or submission language matters. Use the host's attachment or spreadsheet capability for material skipped files, or disclose the gap.
4. **Follow the grant program.** Explain statutory purpose, recurring cycle, funding history, and program-level recipient landscape when linked.
5. **Find comparable awards.** Prefer same program and agency; preserve recipient type, geography, amount, and period differences.
6. **Map likely applicants and partners.** Use prior recipients and direct program evidence, not generic organization similarity.
7. **Identify gaps.** Separate eligibility blockers, missing registrations, cost-share needs, partner needs, and evidence gaps.
8. **Stop at the requested decision.** Hand application drafting to Proposal Workflows only when the user requests an artifact.

## Output contract

Return:

- opportunity purpose, status, deadline, funding, and eligibility
- program lineage and recurring-cycle context
- file-derived requirements and evaluation signals
- comparable awards and recipient landscape
- fit, partnership, and next-action implications requested
- confidence and missing evidence

## Gotchas

- Program authorization or historical funding does not guarantee this notice's funding.
- Past recipients are not automatically likely applicants.
- Eligibility must come from the controlling notice and amendments.
- Do not mix grants, cooperative agreements, and contracts without labeling the instrument.
