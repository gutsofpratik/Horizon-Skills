# Federal Grant Program Deep Dive

Use this reference for one resolved federal assistance program or listing.

## Default procedure

1. **Lock the program.** Capture exact ID/listing, title, agency, objectives, assistance types, eligibility, geography, statutory context, and direct relationships.
2. **Analyze linked opportunities.** Separate active, future, closed, and historical notices and identify recurring cycles or amendments.
3. **Analyze linked awards.** Summarize funding, recipient types, geography, concentration, and representative awards with consistent value basis and time window.
4. **Map recipients.** Resolve exact recipient identities and distinguish prime recipients from subrecipients.
5. **Read program or opportunity files when material.** Follow [Vector-store content retrieval](govtribe-docs-vector-store-content-retrieval.md) with `Add_To_Vector_Store` and `Search_Vector_Store` for exact eligibility, scoring, reporting, or cost-share language. Use the host's attachment or spreadsheet capability for material skipped files, or disclose the gap.
6. **Identify cadence and signals.** Use budget, notice, and award history to infer timing; label the inference and confidence.
7. **Add bounded market context.** Expand adjacent programs only when they answer a named fit, funding, or partnership question.

## Output contract

Return:

- program purpose, owner, instrument types, and eligibility
- opportunity calendar and lifecycle
- award and recipient landscape
- funding cadence and concentration
- fit, partnership, and monitoring implications
- confidence, limitations, and next watch triggers

## Gotchas

- Program ceilings and appropriations are not awarded funding.
- Different assistance types may have different eligibility and reporting rules.
- Award totals need a defined period and deduplication method.
- Related programs are not substitutes for exact program evidence.
