# Evidence-qualified research and business deliverables

## Technical research

Separate a vendor's claim from a confirmed specification and tested compatibility. Cite evidence for each material characteristic with date and exact entity/part identity. Preserve competing candidates, unresolved scope and a concise next evidence request. For crosswalks, use the host's extraction/table capability and maintain a source inventory with stable source ID, exact revision, page/sheet/row identity, extraction status, unresolved gaps and next resume position. Reconcile the full inventory against extracted rows before claiming completeness; search success does not establish completeness. If extraction is unavailable, provide the supported Markdown/CSV rows and label missing coverage.

## Company profiles and graphics

Resolve entity identity with UEI/CAGE, geography and legal name before aggregating. Keep namesakes separate. Distinguish award obligations, ceilings and contract values from company revenue and profit; unknown financials remain unknown. Attribute headcount/revenue claims to their source and date instead of converting them into verified facts. Present qualitative milestones as dated events without an invented growth axis or scoring scale. Quantitative charts require an explicit measure, unit, period and source; subjective ratings require a disclosed rubric.

Synthetic control: Atlas Labs ALPHA01 (Maryland) and BETA02 (Oregon) are separate entities. ALPHA01's $2m/$3m awards do not prove $2m/$3m revenue. A press release claiming 25 staff remains a claim. Founding in 2020 and a first award in 2024 are events, not measured company growth.

## Prospects and contacts

Define hard screens before ranking: required capability, active period at a stated as-of date, clearance evidence, geography and required contact fields. A candidate missing required evidence is unresolved, not qualified. Record reason codes and source dates. Active start/end boundaries must be explicit. Keep company identity and contact identity separate, with separately reconciled counts; do not substitute two contacts for two companies. Missing required email fails qualification; never fabricate an address from a naming convention.

Synthetic control at 2030-06-01: C1 has an active contract, confirmed clearance and two sourced emails; it yields one qualified company and two contacts. C2 expired May 31 and is excluded. C3 has unknown clearance/email and stays unresolved. Keep the full screened inventory beside the qualified subset.

## Past performance

Vehicle access or an award alone does not prove performed work. Require performed scope, agency/customer, period and acceptance/outcome evidence appropriate to the request. Separate confirmed performance, candidate awards requiring evidence, and vehicle-only access. Missing scope stays explicit; do not manufacture a past-performance narrative from a title. Provide a concise evidence-collection table with record, missing fact, source to request, owner and next action.

Synthetic control: V1 is vehicle-only; A1 has helpdesk scope plus confirmed acceptance and supports performance; A2 has an award but unknown performed scope and remains a candidate. Maya requests V1 task orders; Lee requests A2 scope/acceptance. Do not bury those two actions under an elaborate unsupported narrative.

For an evidence-collection brief, reconcile every supplied source ID and its performance/vehicle/candidate classification, missing scope and accountable next action. Use the document contract for required content, formatting and rendered review. Do not impose the full-dossier validator’s five-source minimum or a full requirement-match memo when no target requirement was supplied; padding with invented evidence is a failure.

## Task contract and delivery state

Before drafting or revising, declare the intended reader, decision, exact source inventory/revisions, required content, output format, and acceptance checks. Use `govtribe-document-editing` for its document contract and `govtribe-govcon-writing` for evidence-aware prose when installed, resolving each from its own installed root. If unavailable, apply those checks directly through the host's corresponding document capability.

Open the exact selected original artifact for edits; preserve its source identity, approved text, images, tables and unaffected content. Vector excerpts are research evidence, not an editable original. If original access is unavailable, report the limitation and obtain the exact source before claiming an in-place revision.

Reconcile every supplied source ID to a classification, supporting evidence, missing fact, owner and next action. Report delivery as complete, partial or blocked against the task contract. List executed content/structure checks, rendered visual checks and unresolved work separately. Render the final document/PDF, inspect it at final size, repair issues and repeat. If a host renderer is unavailable, deliver the validated source plus Markdown/CSV and state that visual QA remains unverified. Do not call a structurally valid or partial result a completed requested artifact.
