# Annotated Outline Quality Checks

Use this checklist before returning the final annotated outline.

## Source coverage

- [ ] Source inventory lists every solicitation document, amendment, Q&A, attachment, and GovTribe/customer context source used.
- [ ] Latest amendments/Q&A are reflected.
- [ ] Inaccessible or missing documents are listed in assumptions/gaps.
- [ ] Source IDs are stable and used consistently in matrix and outline.

## Requirement coverage

- [ ] Every mandatory Section L/instruction requirement is mapped to an outline location or listed as unmapped.
- [ ] Every evaluation factor/subfactor is mapped to at least one outline location.
- [ ] Major SOW/PWS/SOO tasks and deliverables are mapped to relevant technical/management sections.
- [ ] Page limits, formatting, file names, volume rules, due dates, questions deadline, and submission portal rules are captured.
- [ ] Required forms, certifications, attachments, pricing templates, and past performance questionnaires are captured.
- [ ] Inferred requirements are labeled as inferred, not stated as solicitation facts.

## Outline quality

- [ ] Outline follows required solicitation structure unless explicitly labeled as inferred.
- [ ] Each major section has purpose, mandatory content, evaluation mapping, evidence needs, source mapping, and open questions.
- [ ] Suggested win themes are tied to available customer/GovTribe evidence or marked `[NEEDS CUSTOMER PROOF]`.
- [ ] Graphics/tables are practical and tied to evaluator needs.
- [ ] Page budget does not exceed solicitation limits; if no limit exists, allocation is labeled as recommended.
- [ ] Writer work packages are actionable.

## Risk and compliance

- [ ] Conflicting instructions are surfaced with source references.
- [ ] No unsupported capabilities, certifications, clearances, staffing, past performance, or pricing claims are invented.
- [ ] Legal/contractual interpretation risks are flagged for contracts/legal review rather than presented as legal advice.
- [ ] Proprietary company information is scoped to the active account.

## Final response quality

- [ ] Executive summary states the outline basis and main risks.
- [ ] Tables are readable in Markdown and do not force full compliance-matrix columns into a portrait document.
- [ ] If the full compliance matrix has more than 12 rows, the Markdown contains only a compact summary and points to the CSV/JSON companion for the complete matrix.
- [ ] The Markdown compliance summary uses 8 or fewer representative/high-risk rows by default.
- [ ] Unmapped/ambiguous requirements are not hidden.
- [ ] Next steps are prioritized and specific.
