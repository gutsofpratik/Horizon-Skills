# Quality checks

Run this checklist before delivering the workbook. If any item fails, fix the workbook and re-check.

## Source and control baseline
- [ ] `Sources` includes every solicitation, attachment, amendment, and Q&A source used.
- [ ] The workbook reflects the latest conformed or amended solicitation text available.
- [ ] `Opportunity Setup` shows the current due date, time, time zone, submission method, and major proposal volumes.

## Requirement Matrix completeness
- [ ] Every working row has a `Req ID`.
- [ ] Every working row has `Source Area`, `Source Citation`, and `Verbatim Requirement`.
- [ ] Rows are split so that separate commands are not buried in one line.
- [ ] `Proposal Action / Interpretation` is a concrete writer task, not a vague restatement.
- [ ] Mandatory and evaluated rows have an `Owner` or are clearly flagged as a gap.
- [ ] High-risk rows identify needed evidence, artifacts, or support contributors.
- [ ] Final proposal location fields are filled for late-stage or update workbooks.

## Section M coverage
- [ ] Every evaluation factor and significant subfactor from Section M appears in `Evaluation Crosswalk`.
- [ ] Each `Evaluation Crosswalk` row has either `Related Req IDs`, a proposal section, or both.
- [ ] Evaluator priorities and discriminators are visible, not buried in notes.

## Submission controls
- [ ] `Submission Checklist` includes deadline, time zone, portal or delivery method, file naming, formatting, amendment acknowledgement, signatures, and required forms.
- [ ] Page limits, font rules, spacing rules, and packaging instructions are captured when present.
- [ ] Technical and price volume separation controls are captured when present.

## Pricing and deliverables
- [ ] `Pricing & Deliverables` includes CLINs, pricing attachments, deliverables, and support files that affect completeness.
- [ ] Any pricing instruction that could invalidate the offer or weaken price evaluation is represented.

## Change control
- [ ] Every amendment or relevant Q&A item is logged in `Amendment Log`.
- [ ] Each impacted requirement row reflects the amendment or Q&A change.
- [ ] Stale pre-amendment guidance is not left active without a superseded note.

## Issues and uncertainty
- [ ] Ambiguities, missing documents, internal assumptions, and required decisions are logged in `Questions-Risks`.
- [ ] Unclear items are flagged as `Question` or `Partial` rather than guessed.
- [ ] If the workbook is partial, the missing source documents are clearly stated.

## Workbook usability
- [ ] Filters and headers are intact on working sheets.
- [ ] Dashboard formulas or counts still work.
- [ ] The workbook is understandable to capture, proposal, pricing, and review leads.
- [ ] There is a concise narrative summary of gaps, risks, and next actions.

## Stop conditions
Do not represent the workbook as complete if any of these are true:
- Section L or equivalent proposal instructions are missing,
- Section M or equivalent evaluation factors are missing,
- the latest amendment set is missing,
- key attachments, forms, or pricing sheets referenced by the RFP are missing.

In those cases, deliver a partial workbook and explicitly list the missing inputs needed to finish it.
