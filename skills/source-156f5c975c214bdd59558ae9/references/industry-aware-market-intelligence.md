# Industry-Aware Market Intelligence

Use this reference alongside the matching market-intelligence workflow when the market slice, saved search, buyer lane, monitor, recompete set, or retrieved records clearly point to an industry. Industry context shapes search lanes and cohort construction; evidence controls the claims.

## Progressive-disclosure rule

1. Load the task-specific market workflow first.
2. Select one primary industry lane below.
3. Load a mission-domain modifier only when it materially changes the market.
4. Do not load every industry reference.

For mixed markets, choose the lane that best explains recurring buyer demand and the dominant value model. Add one secondary lane only when it materially changes cohort construction, competitor archetypes, lifecycle interpretation, or monitor triggers.

## Select the primary lane

- [Products, Supply, and Equipment](./industry-market-products-supply-equipment.md): item identifiers, equipment families, OEM/distributor/integrator archetypes, supply signals, recompetes, and monitors.
- [Digital, IT, Cyber, and Data](./industry-market-digital-it-cyber.md): modernization, SaaS/cloud, software, cyber, data/AI, telecom, platform incumbency, transitions, vehicles, and monitors.
- [Professional and Mission-Support Services](./industry-market-professional-services.md): labor-led buyer lanes, staffing models, incumbent continuity, service vehicles, recompetes, and monitors.
- [Construction, AEC, and Facilities](./industry-market-construction-aec-facilities.md): facility types, trades, facilities commands, geography, phase transitions, construction/maintenance signals, and contamination controls.
- [Mission-Domain Modifiers](./industry-market-mission-domain-modifiers.md): healthcare/life sciences; R&D/scientific; environmental/energy; transportation/aviation/maritime; public safety/emergency response.

A mission modifier supplements the primary lane. It never replaces it.

## Market classification

Resolve:

- primary lane, subtype, and material adjacent lanes
- federal or state/local market and jurisdiction
- buyer and office scope
- acquisition or funding path
- lifecycle window and demand stage
- value basis and unit of analysis

Treat agency, geography, contract type, compliance regime, and asset type as overlays rather than industries.

## Cohort and claim discipline

- Use search bundles and stable filters rather than one broad keyword.
- Separate direct identifiers, structured categories, buyer/vehicle relationships, and semantic recovery passes.
- Label cohort quality as strong, usable with caveats, thin, or contaminated before making market claims.
- Keep federal and state/local lifecycle semantics distinct.
- Distinguish forecast, opportunity, award, IDV/vehicle, transaction, line-item, and file evidence.
- Avoid double counting parent ceilings, child awards, modifications, and transactions.
- Normalize value basis, time window, geography, and inflation/escalation assumptions before comparison.
- State when a trend, concentration, recompete, or timing claim is inferred rather than directly confirmed.

## Base-workflow preservation

- Preserve the base buying-pattern, early-signal, recompete, recurring-monitor, acquisition-target, and exposure workflow.
- Use industry context to sharpen segmentation, representative records, incumbent signals, competitor archetypes, monitor criteria, and contamination controls.
- Do not turn a market scan into a single-record dossier or pursuit qualification unless the user asks; when the companion skills are available, hand selected targets to `govtribe-deep-dive` or `govtribe-capture-workflows`. Otherwise preserve the market result and provide a bounded handoff brief.
- Do not claim inventory, authorization, capacity, profitability, ownership, or transaction intent from government-market evidence alone.

## Current-rule verification

Source dates, program names, thresholds, cooperative rules, and state/local requirements can change. Verify current versions at execution time with the host's provider-neutral web-browsing capability and official sources when they materially affect the market interpretation. If browsing is unavailable, label the item unverified, keep it out of decisive scoring, and provide the official source link for follow-up.
