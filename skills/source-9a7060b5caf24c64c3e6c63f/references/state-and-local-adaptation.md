# State and local adaptation

Keep the writing workflow and replace federal assumptions with the issuing jurisdiction's actual instructions.

1. Resolve the state/local entity, procurement identifier, document type, and current solicitation/addenda.
2. Use its required questions, forms, certifications, evaluation criteria, deadline/time zone, and delivery channel. Check relevant local procurement guidance only when the response needs it.
3. Preserve local terminology and section structure. Do not impose federal Sections L/M, FAR clauses, SAM registration, federal status representations, or federal submission rules without an applicable requirement.
4. Confirm whether federal funding introduces a particular requirement; funding alone does not justify importing every federal procurement rule.
5. Apply the same clear writing, evidence attribution, commitment preservation, and draft/readiness distinctions. Treat local exceptions or missing forms as specific unresolved requirements.

The Oregon supplier guide in `references/research-sources.md` is an example of following local questions, addenda, forms, and submission instructions. It does not establish the rules of another state, county, city, or authority.
