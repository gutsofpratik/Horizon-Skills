# RFI and sources-sought responses

Treat a market-research request as a request for information under its actual instructions. Do not assume that it is an offer, that pricing is prohibited, or that a universal response format applies.

## Answer the actual questions

1. Recover the notice, question set, current attachments, form fields, limits, and company evidence. Reuse an existing proposal-workflow requirement map. Before drafting, classify proposed answers using the evidence states in `references/source-authority-and-claims.md`.
2. Preserve the requested question IDs and order. Give a supported answer for every question; when the answer is unknown, keep that item unresolved in a review draft and identify the missing evidence in the internal handoff. Do not use a negative answer merely to fill the field.
3. Demonstrate relevant capabilities, experience, capacity, availability, delivery constraints, or business status only to the extent supported. Preserve the evidenced scope and performer role; do not fill them from typical industry practices.
4. Provide acquisition or requirement feedback when invited. Explain the operational reason for a suggestion and distinguish it from a government requirement or an agreed solution.
5. Include pricing, vehicle information, or certifications only when requested or relevant and supported. Do not invent a future set-aside, award commitment, or eligibility conclusion.

## Handle surveys and short fields

- Treat required survey fields as deliverables. A capability attachment does not replace unanswered fields unless the instructions allow it.
- Honor the specific word/character convention and attachment count/type rules. Count the final field value, including spaces when specified; exclude labels only if the actual form does.
- Keep essential requested facts when shortening: actor, scope, capacity, timing, and important qualifications. Prefer removing repetition over removing a material limitation.
- Preserve plain-text fields as plain text when requested. Do not insert Markdown headings or extra commentary into a field-only answer.
- Check upload instructions from the current notice or portal guidance; do not generalize limits from another RFI or an old MRAS example.

## Useful capability evidence

Use completed projects, actual role, customer type, period, scale, delivery approach, and supported results to establish relevance. Distinguish existing capacity from an unconfirmed surge plan and verified status from a pending certification.

When a fact is missing, supply the strongest truthful draft and a specific internal question. Keep an incomplete package in review-draft status. Do not add a binding acceptance statement merely because a proposal template contains one.

## Review

Check question coverage, factual support, form-field limits, and consistency between narrative and attachments. Keep any suggested procurement approach clearly identifiable as industry feedback. Do not claim that a response will cause a set-aside or an award. Check each sentence against evidence and approved approaches; remove unsupported claims of either polarity and unapproved company commitments. Unresolved material answers prevent a submission-ready or submittable claim.
