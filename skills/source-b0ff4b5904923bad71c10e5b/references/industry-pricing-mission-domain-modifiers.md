# Industry-Aware Pricing Mission-Domain Modifiers

Load one modifier only when it materially changes the primary economic model. A modifier supplements rather than replaces the primary lane.

## Healthcare, human services, medical, pharmaceutical, and life sciences

Potential pricing units:

- encounter, member/month, case, claim, call, assessment, visit, bed/day, specimen, test, procedure, dose, device, kit, FTE/hour, or fixed program

Additional drivers:

- clinical/credentialed labor, staffing ratios, on-call coverage, credentialing time, malpractice/professional liability, background/exclusion checks, rural travel, language access, data/privacy, clinical systems, quality reporting, patient safety, cold chain, waste, inventory expiry, and continuity
- payer/claims/admin complexity, no-show/utilization, network/provider subcontracting, service area, and surge

Evidence caution:

- BLS may orient wages but not licensed specialty recruiting or regulated reimbursement.
- Product/device line items require exact model/configuration and status.
- Do not infer FDA/CMS/CLIA cost or applicability without source scope.

## R&D, scientific, engineering, testing, and prototyping

Potential model:

- direct labor by discipline
- materials/components/prototypes
- laboratory/test facility/equipment
- subawards/subcontracts/universities
- travel/data/compute
- indirect rates
- fee/cost share
- milestones and uncertainty reserve

Additional drivers:

- technical maturity, experimental iterations, yield/failure, specialized facilities, key personnel, test articles, data/IP rights, publication/export/security, human/animal/biosafety, and prototype transition

Use ranges and milestone scenarios. Do not force uncertain research effort into a single deterministic unit price when the source does not support it.

## Environmental, energy, utilities, and natural resources

Potential units:

- sample, analysis, monitoring well, acre, ton, gallon, cubic yard, disposal load, treatment volume, site, kWh/therm/MW, asset, or guaranteed savings

Additional drivers:

- contaminant/media, investigation uncertainty, laboratory, mobilization, PPE, health/safety, waste profile, transport/disposal, permits, long-term monitoring, seasonality, utility tariff, interconnection, financing, M&V, O&M, and performance guarantee

Separate pass-through disposal/utility cost from contractor service and risk. Use current facility/tariff/quote evidence externally where required.

## Transportation, fleet, transit, aviation, maritime, and mobility

Potential units:

- mile, route, trip, stop, vehicle/hour, vehicle/month, passenger, shipment, hundredweight, ton, container, flight hour, vessel day, or availability

Additional drivers:

- vehicle/asset ownership or lease, utilization, deadhead/repositioning, driver/operator labor, fuel/energy, tolls, maintenance, tires, insurance, permits, terminals, storage, dispatch, safety, accessibility, subcontracted carriers, claims, and surge

Show fuel and volume adjustments separately. Do not compare per-mile, per-route, and per-hour rates without converting through a source-backed operating profile.

## Security, public safety, emergency response, and disaster recovery

Potential units:

- post/hour, officer/hour, site/month, response, standby day, deployed team, vehicle/equipment day, ton/cubic yard removed, shelter bed/day, or fixed activation

Additional drivers:

- credentials/training, shift coverage, overtime, weapons/equipment, vehicles, communications, insurance, background checks, mobilization, staging, lodging/per diem, surge, standby/activation, hazard/PPE, documentation, demobilization, and emergency subcontracting

Model normal, surge, and emergency activation separately. Verify any price controls, emergency authority, local-area preference, or reimbursement documentation from the source.
